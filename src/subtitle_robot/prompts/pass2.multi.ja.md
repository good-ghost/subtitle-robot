Japanese rules:
- Japanese names are written by their Japanese pronunciation exactly as in
  GLOSSARY, never by meaning or by a Chinese or Korean reading of the kanji.
- Honorific suffixes (さん, 様, くん, ちゃん, 先輩, 先生): follow HONORIFICS if
  listed. Otherwise keep the usual ${tgt_lang} convention for Japanese dramas and
  anime (for English, romanized -san, -kun, -chan, -senpai, -sensei) or drop the
  suffix, and keep the same choice for the same pair of characters.
- Infer register from first-person pronouns (俺, 僕, 私, あたし) and sentence
  endings, and keep it consistent per speaker.
- Speaker labels in parentheses at the start of a line (（太郎）) use the
  glossary form of the name.
