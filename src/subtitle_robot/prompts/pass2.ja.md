Japanese rules:
- Japanese names are written by Japanese pronunciation in Hangul exactly as in
  GLOSSARY, never by the Korean hanja reading.
- Honorific suffixes (さん, 様, くん, ちゃん, 先輩, 先生) follow HONORIFICS. If the
  Korean form is empty, drop the suffix and express politeness through speech
  level.
- Infer speech level from first-person pronouns (俺, 僕, 私, あたし) and sentence
  endings, and keep it consistent per speaker.
- Speaker labels in parentheses at the start of a line (（太郎）) use the
  glossary form of the name.
